A REAL LESSON

Connected Speech: “at the”

Help students understand how words connect in natural English.

LISTEN & REPEAT

Say the words separately — then together.

at/æt/
+
the/ðə/
→
at the/ət ðə/

💡 Practice tip

Don't put a big pause between the words. Start slowly, then gradually make the phrase more natural.
